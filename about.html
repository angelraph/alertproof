Alertproof was built in Nigeria for the people who keep its economy moving: traders, POS agents and small shops who get paid by transfer and have seconds to decide whether to trust it.

Why we built it

“Fake alert” is a household phrase in Nigeria. Every trader knows the moment: a customer says “I don send am”, a message lands, the shop is busy, and the goods are already in the bag. Banks tell you to check your account, not the message. That advice is right, but at 6pm with five customers waiting, nobody opens a banking app for every sale.

We noticed something simple. A fraudster can copy everything about an alert except one number: your balance. Every real alert continues your balance from the last one. Checking that takes arithmetic, not trust, so we built a tool that does the arithmetic for you.

What we believe

  • AI reads, rules decide. A model should never be the one saying your money is real.
  • Your data stays yours. The balance that protects you belongs on your device, not in a cloud.
  • Honest beats confident. When we can’t prove it, we say CAN’T TELL, and we publish our mistakes.
  • Open by default. Open-weight Gemma, open source code, open test results.
