Know the game

Four tricks. One answer.

These are the fake-payment tricks traders talk about every day. Each one breaks a rule that arithmetic can check.

Trick 1 · Fake-alert apps

The SMS that looks perfect

“Check your phone, e don enter.”

Apps that send a bank-looking SMS with any amount you type. The words are perfect. The balance is invented.

→ Alertproof: the balance doesn’t follow your chain. NO RELEASE.

Trick 2 · Edited screenshots

“Transfer Successful”

“See am for my phone, I don send am.”

A screenshot edited, reused, or of a transfer that later fails. It is a picture, not money.

→ Alertproof: wait for your own alert. It tells you exactly what it must say.

Trick 3 · The old alert

Yesterday’s money, again

“Abeg check your messages.”

A real alert from earlier, forwarded or shown again to look like a new payment.

→ Alertproof: you already received this exact alert. NO RELEASE.

Trick 4 · Wrong account

Paid, just not to you

“Na your account number I use.”

A genuine alert or receipt for somebody else’s account, often with a similar name.

→ Alertproof: this alert is for an account ending 432. Yours ends 4821.
