# Alertproof evaluation

Generated by `node eval/run.mjs`. 70 alerts per model across 10 formats (SMS, push notifications and emails from GTBank, Access, Zenith, UBA, First Bank, OPay, Moniepoint and PalmPay). For every format the trader already has a morning of genuine alerts in their book; each case is one new alert arriving.

Cases per format: two genuine credits (one with kobo), one debit, a fake with an invented balance, a fake with a round balance, an alert for someone else's account, and a replay of a real alert from earlier that day.

The model only reads. The verdict is arithmetic in `lib/chain.js`.
